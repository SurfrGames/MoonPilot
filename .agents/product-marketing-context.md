# Moon Pilot — product marketing context and earning plan

_Last updated 2026-11-10. Marketing skills read this file first._

## Product
- Free browser game (also Android via Capacitor, planned): fly a real-physics Moon rocket through
  four missions and race the ghost of a neural network that taught itself the same flights.
- Hook: "Can you out-fly an AI that learned from scratch?" Proof asset: replays of the AI at
  0%, 3%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 100% of training (`rocket-lander/orbit3d.html`).
- Built: 4 missions, controls, HUD, scoring, AI ghosts, logo. Not built yet: tutorial, phone
  flight layout pass, daily challenge, leaderboard, ads, Android build, store pages.
- Maker: solo student developer, zero budget, needs income. Repo: SurfrGames/MoonPilot (private).

## Audience
1. Space/physics game players (orbital-mechanics fans). 2. People curious about AI learning to
play. 3. Casual web-portal players (largest group, least patient: need a tutorial).

## Monetisation (decided 2026-11-10)
Income follows plays, so the game stays free everywhere and earns three ways:
1. **Web portals with ad revenue share** (CrazyGames first, then others) — expected main earner.
2. **Android**: free with rewarded ads (optional ad for a retry or hint) plus a one-time
   "remove ads + supporter pack" purchase, low price.
3. **itch.io pay-what-you-want** with a small suggested price — a tip jar and home base, not
   the main income.
No paywalled missions at launch. Supporter extras (rocket skins, name in credits) are to build.

## Channels
- Owned: itch.io page + devlog, later a Discord.
- Rented: short videos (YouTube Shorts, Reels) of the AI learning; Reddit; X.
- Borrowed: web game portals, Show HN, r/reinforcementlearning, r/WebGames, r/IndieGaming,
  YouTubers who cover AI-learns-to-play.

## Launch phases
1. Friends test (now): 5-10 people play while watched; fix where they get stuck.
2. itch.io launch (week of 2026-11-16): page, trailer, devlog, first video, Reddit/HN posts.
3. Play closed test: 12 testers for 14 days (Google rule for new personal accounts).
4. Portal submissions (reviews take time) and Play public release.
5. Every later feature (daily challenge, leaderboard, new missions) is its own small launch.

## Build order (by effect on plays and income)
1. First-flight tutorial and on-screen guidance. 2. Phone flight layout. 3. Daily challenge +
shareable score card. 4. itch.io page copy, screenshots, 30-45 s trailer. 5. Portal ad SDK and
Android build with rewarded ads. 6. Online leaderboard.
